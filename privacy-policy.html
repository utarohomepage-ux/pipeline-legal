Pipeline · Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-27

Pipeline is a personal automation tool built and operated by a single individual developer to publish video content that the developer creates and owns to the developer's own social media accounts, including TikTok. This policy explains what data Pipeline touches when it connects to TikTok, and what it does not do.

01

Who this applies to

Pipeline is not a public app or service. It has exactly one user: the developer who built it, publishing their own content to their own accounts. No one else signs in to Pipeline, and Pipeline never acts on behalf of any TikTok account other than the one the developer has explicitly authorized.

02

Information Pipeline accesses

When the developer connects Pipeline to a TikTok account, Pipeline accesses only:

  • Basic profile information for the authorized account (display name, avatar, open ID) — used solely to confirm which account is connected.
  • The ability to upload and publish video content to that same authorized account.

Pipeline does not read, collect, or store content, profile data, or analytics belonging to any other TikTok user.

03

What Pipeline does not do

  • Pipeline does not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties.
  • Pipeline does not use TikTok data for advertising, profiling, or analytics.
  • Pipeline does not access direct messages, followers, or any data outside the single authorized account's own posting permissions.
04

Data retention

Pipeline stores the authorization token for the connected account only for as long as needed to publish content, on infrastructure the developer controls directly. The developer can revoke Pipeline's access at any time from TikTok's account settings, which immediately invalidates the stored token.

05

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a revised date above.
