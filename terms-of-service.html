Pipeline is a personal automation tool built and operated by a single individual developer to publish video content that the developer creates and owns to the developer's own social media accounts, including TikTok. These terms describe the scope of that use.

01

What Pipeline is

Pipeline renders short-form video from the developer's own source footage and publishes it to the developer's own accounts on YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok, on a schedule the developer sets. It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and is not a platform for other people's content.

02

Single-user scope

Pipeline connects to exactly one TikTok account, authorized directly by the developer who owns that account. No other person or account is granted posting access through Pipeline, and Pipeline does not act as an intermediary between TikTok and any third party.

03

Use of TikTok's platform

Content published through Pipeline is subject to TikTok's own Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. Pipeline uses TikTok's Content Posting API strictly to upload and publish the developer's own video content to the developer's own authorized account.
